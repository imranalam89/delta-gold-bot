            arthur_macro.py
          

          

            How the 16 agents distinguish untradeable "Barbed Wire Chop" from a wide 1H sideways candle range that contains clean, profitable 1M/5M internal swings:
          


          

            

              
1. Candle Overlap & Choppiness Index (ARTHUR)

              

                ARTHUR computes the 14-period Choppiness Index (CHOP) and an Overlap Ratio over the last 6 Higher-Timeframe (1H/15M) candles:
                Overlap = Sum(Shared High-Low Range) / Total Range. When CHOP > 61.8 or Overlap > 70%, ARTHUR broadcasts regime = "HTF_RANGE" and locks out MARCUS (Breakout Trader) so the desk never buys tops or sells bottoms of a range.
              

            


            

              
2. The "Room-to-Breathe" ATR Filter (DAVID + ETHAN)

              

                When 1H candles are overlapping, ARTHUR measures the total width of the 1H range (Range_High - Range_Low) and compares it to the 1M/5M Average True Range (ATR_5m):
              

              
    
                
  • Dead Micro-Chop (NO TRADE): If 1H Range Width < 4 × ATR_5m (e.g., total box is only $2.50 wide on Gold), DAVID vetoes all agents. There is no room to achieve a 1:2.5 R:R before hitting opposing chop.

  • Tradeable Internal Trend (SCALP MODE ACTIVE): If 1H Range Width ≥ 6 × ATR_5m (e.g., $12.00 wide box on Gold), ARTHUR activates INTERNAL_RANGE_PING_PONG mode!
